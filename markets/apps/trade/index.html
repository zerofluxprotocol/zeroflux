How these perps work
IndexThe 7-day average of what each model's OpenRouter usage cost, in USD per day. A new day lands after 00:00 UTC. Not the model's revenue.
MarkSet by trading on the order book: where traders think demand is heading.
FundingPaid between longs and shorts while mark and index differ, so the perp tracks the index.
CapsOpen interest per market is capped at 25% of the model's 7-day spend, so pushing the index costs more than it can win.
SafetyMissing data, or a day that moves the index more than 25%, puts a market in close-only.
StatusNEAR testnet, paper trading only. No real money.
